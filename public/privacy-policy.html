BrandOps Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026

1. Who this applies to

Anyone who uses a BrandOps web, browser-extension, or packaged mobile build.

2. What BrandOps stores on your device

BrandOps keeps your workspace (tasks, pipeline, content, settings, and exports you create) in browser or packaged-app storage on your device. Workspace data leaves your device only when you export it or use a feature that clearly names a configured external service, such as hosted Ask.

The current account gate records an on-device access preference and a provider-labelled preview identity. It does not authenticate with Google, Apple, GitHub, LinkedIn, or email.

3. Account access in the current build

Google-, Apple-, GitHub-, LinkedIn-, and email-labelled buttons currently choose a local preview identity only. BrandOps does not open an OAuth authorization flow, send a magic-link email, obtain a provider token, verify the displayed email address, or create a server-side session. Provider access and refresh tokens are not part of workspace storage or exports. If a future release adds federated authentication, this policy will be updated before that feature is enabled.

Provider Use in BrandOps
Apple Local preview label only; Apple sign-in is not enabled
Email Local preview label only; magic-link email is not enabled
Google Local preview label only; Google OAuth is not enabled
GitHub Local preview label only; GitHub OAuth is not enabled
LinkedIn Local preview label only; LinkedIn OAuth is not enabled

4. Network requests

Besides browser or packaged-app services, BrandOps may contact:

  • LinkedIn pages you visit, where the extension content script may read the current profile URL, name, role, and company to prefill its companion form. Those fields are saved to the local workspace only when you use the capture action.
  • Optional hosted AI bridge: when you enable hosted AI and configure approved inference or embeddings URLs, BrandOps may send Assistant prompts, AI-pipeline messages, selected workspace context, an optional compressed résumé grounding artifact, and content-library titles and bodies selected for embedding sync to those URLs. The API key is stored in device-local credential storage, excluded from workspace exports, and transmitted to the approved endpoint in an authorization header. Packaged mobile builds do not yet use an operating-system keychain.
  • An optional rules URL explicitly configured by the person who built or deployed the app.
  • Stripe checkout or billing-portal pages only when you choose an externally configured billing link.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Sign out

Dashboard Sign out clears the local account-access preference. It does not revoke a provider session because the current build does not create one. Removing the extension or packaged app deletes locally stored data subject to browser, operating-system, and backup behavior.

6. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the product changes. The effective date above will be revised. For questions, use the support or privacy contact supplied by the store listing, website, or organization that distributed your BrandOps build.
